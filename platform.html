OUR PLATFORM

More Than a Marketplace. A Connected Ecosystem.

Teneyasiree aims to bring different everyday needs together through one connected digital platform. From discovering local products to finding services and connecting with other people, our goal is to make local opportunities easier to access.

Local Marketplace

A place where individuals and sellers can list permitted products they own and where buyers can discover products from their surrounding communities.

Flexible Delivery

Our planned delivery options include separate delivery partners and seller-managed delivery with seller-defined charges.

Transport Booking

A planned service to help people discover and arrange suitable transport options, including tractors and other vehicles.

Labour Services

We aim to connect people who need work assistance with individuals offering suitable labour services.

Community Connections

Our vision is to make communication and connections between buyers, sellers and service providers easier.

Farmer Guidance

We aim to provide useful guidance and information that can support farmers in making informed decisions.

OUR APPROACH

Designed Around Everyday Needs.

  • Buyers and sellers can participate in the marketplace.
  • One person can be both a buyer and a seller.
  • Flexible delivery arrangements are part of our vision.
  • COD and UPI payment options are planned.
  • Transport and labour services are part of the proposed ecosystem.
  • Communication between buyers and sellers is an important goal.
  • Local language accessibility is part of our vision.
