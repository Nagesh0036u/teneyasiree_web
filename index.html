A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Get to know Teneyasiree.

A few simple answers about the vision behind our platform.

What is Teneyasiree?

Teneyasiree is developing a multi-category digital platform intended to connect buyers, sellers, service providers and local communities.

Who can use the platform?

The platform is being designed for people and businesses interested in buying, selling products or accessing services.

What services are part of the vision?

The wider vision includes a local marketplace, delivery connections, transport services and labour services.

Are the applications available now?

The applications and features described on this website are part of the Teneyasiree development vision. Availability will be announced when the relevant services are launched.
