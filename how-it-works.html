WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

A Platform Designed for Different People.

For Buyers

Discover products, explore available options and connect with sellers.

For Sellers

List permitted products you own and connect with potential buyers.

For Service Providers

Connect with people looking for transport, labour and other local services.

A participant may take different roles. For example, the same person can be both a buyer and a seller.

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY CONNECTIONS

Cutting-edge technology, built for Bharat

We use the same technology as the world's leading apps, and design it for villages, towns and cities: low-cost phones, patchy networks, and people who prefer to speak rather than type.
