ABOUT TENEYASIREE

A Vision Built Around People and Possibilities.

Teneyasiree is a developing multi-category digital platform created with the goal of connecting buyers, sellers, service providers and communities. We believe that technology can make local opportunities easier to discover and access.

Our vision goes beyond buying and selling. We aim to create a platform where individuals can explore products, connect with service providers, arrange transportation and discover useful opportunities within their communities.
